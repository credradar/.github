# Support

## Data questions and corrections

CredRadar publishes every figure with its source. If you believe a figure in a CredRadar dataset is wrong, please follow the corrections policy at https://credradar.org/corrections/ or email hello@credradar.org — corrections are reviewed against the school's own published documents.

## Using the datasets

Datasets here are licensed CC BY-NC 4.0: attribution required, no commercial use. Citation guidance is in each repository's README.
